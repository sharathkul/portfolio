Portfolio visual sources, 2026-10-28

fragmented-work.png and connected-work.png
Created with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool for this portfolio.
Purpose: Matching tactile paper landscapes, moving from interrupted paths to connected cobalt bridges, as a visual accompaniment to Sharath's account of learning healthcare workflows and connecting the work.
Format: 1536 x 1024 transparent PNG.
These are conceptual illustrations, not screenshots of a healthcare product or claims about patient outcomes.
Exact generation prompts and tool provenance are preserved in the project output folder: outputs/art-direction/PROMPTS.txt.
